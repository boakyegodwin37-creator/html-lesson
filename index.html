About

Arsenal Football Club is a professional football club based in North London, England.The club competes in the English Premier League, the highest level of English men's football. Created in 1886 in Woolwich, South East London, England by workers at the Royal Arsenal munitions factory.

They then moved to North London in 1913 and situated a few miles from a club called Totenham Hotspur who will later become Arsenal's rivals.Whenever there is match between Arsenal and spurs its called a "North London Derby". Workers created Arsenal to have fun outside their working hours, to socialise and build teamwork and to form and compete against other local football clubs.Today, Arseanl is the biggest club in England.Arsenal
